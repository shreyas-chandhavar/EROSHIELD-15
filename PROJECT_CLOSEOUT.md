# Project closeout

## Final status

EROSHIELD-15 is closed at the **preliminary design and aerodynamic screening** stage.

Completed:
- public IEA 15-MW outer-blade reconstruction/reference workflow;
- conformal outer-blade leading-edge shield concept;
- released Rev-A parametric rotor-impact screening;
- mass and centrifugal-load screening;
- average bond-shear screening;
- fixed-span placement study and station-specific polar upgrade;
- literature-informed V2 aerodynamic sensitivity model;
- numerical convergence/clipping diagnostics;
- basic PLM/change-control documentation.

Not completed:
- Phase-12 feathered-termination redesign;
- geometry-specific wind-tunnel or CFD validation of the 2 mm shield;
- adhesive peel/fracture qualification;
- structural FEA of the final attachment;
- rain-erosion/delamination/bending qualification;
- full power-curve/AEP integration.

## Why the project stops here

The project already demonstrates concept development, CAD, BEM modelling, uncertainty handling,
screening calculations and configuration control. Further work would require disproportionately
more time for physical validation and detailed attachment development, so the study is intentionally
closed and preserved as a preliminary engineering portfolio project.

## Most defensible engineering conclusions

1. A leading-edge protection concept can be beneficial under moderate/severe erosion, but the
   aerodynamic penalty of the protection itself can dominate under mild erosion.
2. Abrupt-step termination is the least attractive screened protection analogue.
3. The protection penalty must be treated independently from erosion loss; recovery should be
   calculated as an output rather than prescribed.
4. The V2 BEM model converged for all 252 solved combinations, while axial-induction clipping
   remains a documented numerical limitation.
5. The actual 2 mm EROSHIELD geometry is not experimentally validated; tape/chamfer/step cases
   are analogue sensitivity branches only.
